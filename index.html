Who We Are

Sharing Faith & Empowering Communities

Amazing Media Uganda is a Christian media ministry based in Kampala, committed to carrying the glorious message of the Gospel of Jesus Christ to the ends of the earth.

Using television, radio, internet livestreaming, and community crusades, we speak truth, hope, and love into the lives of believers and seekers alike. We believe in active service, theological depth, and spiritual freedom.
