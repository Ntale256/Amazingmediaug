Know Us Better

Our Mission & Faith

Dedicated to proclaiming the name of Jesus Christ, building spiritual foundations, and serving the Ugandan community.

Our Mission

To produce and broadcast spiritually nourishing media, preach the uncompromised Word of God, and equip believers to serve their local communities effectively.

Our Vision

To see a transformed generation of Christians in East Africa, rooted in scriptural truth, walking in love, and utilizing modern tools to make disciples globally.

Statement of Faith

We believe in the Holy Trinity, the divinity of Jesus Christ, salvation by grace through faith, the authority of the Holy Scriptures, and the fellowship of believers.

Our History

How We Started

Amazing Media Uganda began with a simple burden: to leverage digital advancements to ensure the gospel is accessible to everyone in Uganda, especially the youth who spend their time online.

What started as a small group recording devotionals in a backroom in Kampala has grown into a structured media house. Today, we stream weekly church services, edit gospel music, capture local church events, and partner in community charity projects.

We remain focused on our core directive: to spread the light of Jesus Christ and build a supportive digital sanctuary where people can find comfort, wisdom, and fellowship.
